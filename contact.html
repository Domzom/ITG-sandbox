Your Friendly Neighborhood GM's

Imogene Taylor (she/her)

With over 30 years of experience in TTRPG's, Imogene Taylor has run after-school enrichment for kids for the past four years with Thurgood Marshall's D&D Club. Her passion for helping kids grow through developing their own personal myths within their community is evident through the praise she's gotten from teachers and parents alike.

Adi Stein (they/she)

Running D&D since 2018, Adi is enthusiastic to be your expert guide through a variety of personal adventures! They specialize in a range of settings from solarpunk to science fantasy and love finding new ways to tell tales. To her, a failed roll is just a chance to tell a different story <3.
