Your Friendly Neighborhood GM's

Imogene Cith (she/her)

For the past four years Imogene has run after-school enrichment for kids with Thurgood Marshall's D&D Club. Her passion for helping kids grow and tell their own myths as a community is evident through the praise she's gotten from teachers and parents alike.

Adi Stein (they/she)

Running D&D 5e since 2018, Adi is a passionate guide to your personal myths! They specialize in a range of settings from solarpunk to science fantasy and love finding new ways to tell tales. To her, a failure is just a chance to tell a different story <3.
