Our Story

The company started as Imagination Tailor Gaming, with Imogene running afterschool and school-break enrichment for kids in Seattle's Central District. In Summer 2026, Adi joined the team and we began transforming into a nonprofit and expanding our reach to serve more communities across Seattle. Today we are Imagination Table Games.

Our philosophy is to use personal myth making that happens during D&D to offer children opportunities to wrestle with important social and ethical dilemmas through the safety of abstraction. We focus heavily on Social Emotional Education and the Respectful Cooperative Creativity of D&D.

D&D is a unique game/hobby because of all its different creative aspects! Players draw characters, develop acting skills, and learn group collaboration skills. Gameplay encourages students to build mental math skills and reading comprehension. The social skills and problem solving experience provided by D&D provides life-long benefits.

What am I paying for?

Running a D&D game famously takes a lot of work and experience to go smoothly. For many this hobby is a labor of love scrounged together amongst scraps of free time and ran purely on the love of the game. Yet many games fall apart or meet infrequently without a professional running them due to the the enormous imbalance of time and labor that goes into running it as opposed to being a player.

By buying a ticket, you are allowing us to dedicate time to crafting you/your group's personal stories to play and experiment in. We are writing an interactive story for you to be featured in. We are experts not only in the magic of story-telling, but also well versed in extensive sets of rules and mechanics in addition to being professionals at group management and conflict resolution.

How does my donation help?

Donating helps us lower our prices and provide scholarship seats to make sure as many kids as possible get to play, regardless of economic background. In donating, you are helping contribute to our pool of resources, from table tools to kickstarter investments that push the edge of D&D. You are supporting the power of story-telling to build up senses of self and community that empower and motivate change beyond the table.
